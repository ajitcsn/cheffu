# Cheffu logo generation prompt

Transform Aanya into a polished head-and-shoulders chibi character. Preserve her warm dark-brown Indian skin, dark brown eyes, thick natural eyebrows, long textured black ponytail, turmeric-yellow kurta, small round gold stud earrings, and friendly confidence. Add an oversized white chef's toque. Use a front three-quarter view, an expressive smile, clean charcoal outlines, flat vector-friendly colour, and restrained cel shading.

The face, hat, and ponytail should form a strong square app-icon silhouette that remains recognisable at 32–48 px. Use turmeric yellow, saffron orange accents, cream white, coriander green, and dark charcoal. Keep the background genuinely transparent.

Do not include text, utensils, a border, a national flag, a watermark, hanging earrings, or photorealism. Keep Aanya clearly adult while using playful chibi proportions.
